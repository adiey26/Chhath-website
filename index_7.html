    यह क्या है

    चार दिन का पर्व, चार अलग रोशनी। नहाय-खाय की सुबह से खरना की दीयेवाली रात, संध्या अर्घ्य के डूबते सूरज से उषा अर्घ्य की भोर तक — हर दिन अपने समय के गीत खुद चलाता है। अर्घ्य का समय आपके शहर का, अपने आप। ऊपर पानी की रेखा खींचिए, दिन आगे बढ़ेगा।

    Setup — edit CONFIG at the top of the script

    • Playlists. Four YouTube playlist IDs in CONFIG.playlists, one per parv day. Or one in CONFIG.singlePlaylist. Empty = simulation, as now.
    • Location. IP lookup on load, no permission prompt — ipwho.is with geojs.io as backup, then Patna as last resort. सटीक asks the browser for exact coordinates; बदलें opens the city picker. I could not reach those two endpoints from my sandbox — verify them on Pages.
    • Times are computed, not fetched. NOAA solar algorithm, ~15 lines, works anywhere on earth for any year. Checked against published Patna / Delhi / Kolkata / Varanasi figures.
    • Illustrations. img/parv-1.webp … parv-4.webp, 1600×900, horizon at 66% height. Missing files leave the SVG showing.
    • Sound files. Two needed: sound/ghat-water.mp3 (seamless loop, 20–60s) and sound/shankh.mp3 (one blow, 2–4s). Missing files disable their toggle with a tooltip rather than failing silently. freesound.org has CC-licensed options — check each licence individually.
    • दीये is visual, not audio. Lamps have no sound. The toggle lights the diyas already drawn in the scene and overrides whatever the day arc was doing.
    • Hidden player is a deliberate trade. YouTube's terms expect a visible player at 200×200 minimum; this parks it at 1px off-screen for audio-only. Common practice, not compliant, enforced at YouTube's discretion. If a takedown ever matters more than the design, switching to a small visible player is a ten-line change.
    • Still fake: the listener count.
    • GitHub Pages: YouTube embeds, geolocation and the IP fetch all need a real HTTPS origin. file:// will fail on all three.
